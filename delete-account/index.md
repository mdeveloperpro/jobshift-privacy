# Delete your Jobshift account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the **Jobshift** app, operated by Bpdycalc.

## How to request deletion

**Option 1: Email**
Send an email to bodycalc.help@gmail.com with the subject **"Delete my account"**. Write from the email address registered on your Jobshift account, and include your full name. If you are an employer, include your company name.

**Option 2: In the app**
Open Jobshift, go to **Profile → Report a problem**, choose **Other**, and write "Delete my account".

We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before we delete it. We will delete your account within [30] days of confirming your request.

## What is deleted

- Your login (email and password)
- Your name, phone number, city and college (students)
- Your company name, contact person, phone number and business address (employers)
- Your student ID photo, or your employer business proof
- Reports and messages you sent us

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Records of shifts you worked and payments made to you** (dates, hours, amounts and UPI reference numbers). We keep these for 2 years for accounting, tax and dispute purposes, as required by law. They are not used for anything else.
- **Shifts posted by an employer** may stay on record, with the company name removed or anonymised, if students have worked on them and payments are still on record.
- **Backups** of our database are overwritten within [30] days, so deleted data disappears from them after that period.

## Partial deletion

If you want us to delete only some data, such as your ID photo, send us an email saying which data. We will tell you if we need to keep anything for legal reasons.
